When Is Cyber Monday 2026?

The online-only encore, three days later.

Cyber Monday 2026 falls on Monday, November 30, 2026 — the Monday after Black Friday. It was created in 2005 as the online counterpart to Black Friday's in-store rush, and it's now one of the biggest online sales days of the year.

In practice the two have merged into a single four-day weekend: most retailers keep Black Friday pricing running straight through the weekend and into Cyber Monday, with the deepest online-exclusive discounts typically appearing on Monday itself. If you miss a Black Friday deal, check again on Cyber Monday before paying full price.

What Is Black Friday?

The biggest shopping event of the year — and how it got its name.

Black Friday is the biggest shopping event of the year in the United States, held the day after Thanksgiving. The name originated in Philadelphia in the 1960s, when police described the overwhelming post-Thanksgiving traffic and crowds as "Black Friday." Retailers later embraced the term, reframing it as the day stores finally "go into the black" — turning a profit after months of operating at a loss.

What began as a single day of in-store doorbusters has evolved into a weeks-long online shopping season. Today, deals typically start in early November, peak on Black Friday itself, and continue through the weekend into Cyber Monday.

When do deals actually start?

Most retailers now launch Black Friday pricing during the first two weeks of November. On the day itself, online sales typically go live at midnight to 6 a.m. local time, and the best limited-stock "doorbuster" items sell out within the first few hours.
