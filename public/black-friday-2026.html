Black Friday 2026: The Full Season Timeline

Black Friday 2026 falls on Friday, November 27, with Cyber Monday following on November 30. But the shopping period around those two dates runs about six weeks, from quiet October previews to December clearance. Here is how that stretch usually unfolds, and which dates are worth putting on your calendar.

The Season, Phase by Phase

Early October is preview season. Retailers publish gift guides, category pages and "coming soon" lists, usually without prices attached. Mid-to-late October brings the first leaked ad scans and a scattering of early discounts, often on TVs, laptops and headphones. November is where the volume arrives: member access first, then the heaviest discounting in the final ten days before the 27th.

Each phase serves a different purpose. Previews are for building a list. October leaks are for setting a target price. November early access is where stock and color options are widest, which matters more than the last few percent of discount.

One thing to unlearn: waiting for the day itself rarely helps online. Many prices go live days or weeks earlier, and the deepest cuts are frequently on limited stock that clears before Friday morning.

When Black Friday Ads Usually Appear

Ad timing varies by retailer, but the pattern is fairly stable. Leaked scans tend to surface in the last week of October and the first week of November. Official ads generally publish one to two weeks before Thanksgiving, with the largest chains leaning earlier and smaller retailers later.

A growing number of stores skip the printed ad altogether. They announce deals through apps, email lists and membership programs instead, which means announcements can arrive later and change faster than a printed page ever did.

Treat anything published before the official release as provisional. Prices, bundles and door hours can all shift. Build your shortlist from previews, then confirm details against the retailer's own announcement before you commit.

Preview, Confirmed, Live: Three Labels

Three states matter when you read any sale schedule. Preview means the retailer has said a sale is coming but has not named a price. Confirmed means a dated start time and a specific discount. Live means you can actually put the item in a cart at that price.

Most timelines blend these without saying so. A store can advertise "Black Friday starts November 27" while individual items are already discounted, and a preview price can vanish the moment the sale opens. Check the product page rather than the banner.

Start times also differ by channel. Online sales often open at midnight Eastern, while in-store hours follow local time. If you are comparing regions or planning around a time zone, check which clock the posted start time refers to.

Building a Timeline You Will Use

Start with a list of the ten to twenty things you genuinely intend to buy. Record the current price for each in mid-October, then set an alert for a target price you would actually pay. That single habit outperforms scrolling deal feeds every year.

Add dated checkpoints: review ads in late October, join any membership program in early November if early access matters to you, and place online orders before the final week if delivery timing is tight. Shipping cutoffs for standard delivery generally land in mid-December.

Put the dates in your own calendar app — ad season, November 27, November 30 — and note return deadlines next to each purchase. A written timeline is what keeps a six-week sale season from turning into six weeks of browsing.

After November 27: The Tail

The weekend between Black Friday and Cyber Monday — November 28 and 29, 2026 — is usually a continuation rather than a new event. Many retailers hold the same prices through Monday, and some extend them further.

Cyber Monday on November 30 tends to favor online-only categories, software, subscriptions and marketplace sellers. After that, discounts do not disappear. Apparel, toys and accessories often return to similar prices in mid-December as stores clear inventory before the year ends.

Return windows deserve the same attention as prices. Extended holiday policies usually begin in early-to-mid November, but the exact start date and cutoff depend entirely on the retailer, and the clock often runs from the purchase date rather than Christmas.

FAQ

When is Black Friday 2026?

Friday, November 27, 2026 — the day after US Thanksgiving. Cyber Monday falls on November 30, 2026.

When do Black Friday 2026 ads come out?

Leaked ads typically appear in late October, and official ads generally publish one to two weeks before Thanksgiving. The exact timing depends on the retailer.

Do Black Friday sales start at midnight?

It depends on the store. Online sales commonly open at midnight Eastern time, while in-store hours follow local time. Some retailers start days earlier for members.

How long do Black Friday sales last in 2026?

Many run from the week before Black Friday through Cyber Monday, and some extend into early December. Durations vary by retailer and category.

Is it worth waiting for Black Friday to buy?

Only if you have tracked the price first. Some categories hit their lowest point in October or early November, and stock on the deepest discounts is often limited.
